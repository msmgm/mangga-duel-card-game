Mangga Duel is an original mango-themed card battler where you duel against an AI opponent in strategic head-to-head rounds. The deck features 36 mango cards across 4 unique varieties (Manalagi, Harum Manis, Gedong Gincu, Apel) numbered 1-9, plus 2 powerful Golden Mango cards worth 10. Both sides start with 10 HP and 5 cards in hand. Each round, play a card from your hand or press keys 1-5; the higher card value wins the round and deals damage equal to the difference in card values. Refill your hand each round from the deck and outsmart the AI to claim victory!
